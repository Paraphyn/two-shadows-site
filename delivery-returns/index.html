Changed your mind? Cancelling within 14 days

You can cancel your order within 14 days of the day you receive it, without giving a reason.

  1. Tell us by email at info@twoshadows.co.uk. You can use the model form below, but you don’t have to.
  2. Send the unopened jar(s) back within 14 days of telling us, to: [returns address]. Unless the item is faulty or wrong, you pay the return postage.
  3. We’ll refund the product price and standard delivery within 14 days of receiving the return, or of you showing us proof of postage, whichever is sooner.

Jars are sealed for hygiene. Once a seal is broken, the 14-day right to cancel no longer applies to that jar, but our guarantee does.
