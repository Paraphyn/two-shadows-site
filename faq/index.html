What are Gut & Digestion chews?

Soft chews for dogs made with Bacillus velezensis, a live gut-flora culture, plus prebiotic inulin and natural fibres. They’re a complementary feed designed to support healthy digestion.

What is Bacillus velezensis?

A naturally occurring, spore-forming bacterium. The strain we use, DSM 15544, is listed as zootechnical additive 4b1820, a gut flora stabiliser: it helps keep the good bacteria in your dog’s gut in balance.

Does it contain probiotics?

Yes. Bacillus velezensis DSM 15544 is listed as a zootechnical additive at 7.50 × 10¹¹ CFU per kg.

Why a soft chew?

Most dogs take it like a treat, so there’s no powder to mix and no tablet to hide in food. That makes the daily routine easier to keep up.

How many should I give?

It depends on weight: under 10 kg, one chew a day; 10–30 kg, two; 30–45 kg, three; over 45 kg, four. The guide is on every jar. Not sure? Email us your dog’s weight.

Is it right for my dog?

Gut & Digestion is for dogs over 12 weeks old. If your dog is unwell, on medication or has a health condition, have a word with your vet first.

Can puppies, pregnant or nursing dogs have it?

Draft · confirm with manufacturer Puppies can have it from 12 weeks of age, at the amount for their weight. If your dog is pregnant or nursing, ask your vet before starting.
