Why this strain

Bacillus velezensis DSM 15544

Not all gut-flora cultures are alike. We chose one specific, catalogued strain, so you know exactly what’s in every chew.

A named, deposited strain
DSM 15544 is its reference number in the German collection of microorganisms (DSMZ), so the culture can be traced and identified.
Spore-forming by nature
